Shiwa includes a local CPython 3.12 runtime and Python inference dependencies.
Their license and copyright files are retained within python/ and the installed
site-packages/*.dist-info directories. PyTorch, SentenceTransformers,
Transformers, NumPy, Pillow and their transitive dependencies retain their
original respective licenses. The runtime is the uv Python standalone build.
